Clinical Workflow

How It Works

Step 01
Digital Authentication

Patient authenticates using ABHA ID, Aadhaar OTP, or facial recognition at the kiosk landing screen.

Step 02
Document Scan & Intake

Scans handwritten prescriptions and enters primary symptoms with language preference.

Step 03
4 Adaptive Questions

Gemini AI formulates 4 specific diagnostic questions with voice dictation & text options.

Step 04
Summary & PDF Export

Generates Open Page Clinical Summary with NAMASTE codes, risk score, 1-click PDF download & doctor assignment.
